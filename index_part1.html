Solver models (approximate, documented)

A · Fast (Dijkstra)Fill time = shortest travel time over the cavity graph. Edge cost t = ℓ/ū with ū ∝ S·|∇p| lumped into a permeability-like weight; wall proximity adds drag penalty (wall tightness × 1/dist). Fiber load scales viscosity η=η₀(1+4vf).
B · Hele-Shaw∇·(S∇p)=0 in cavity, S=h²_gap/(12η) (gap in m), Dirichlet p=p_inj on gates/charge, p=0 on exposed flow front, Neumann elsewhere. Solved by red-black Gauss-Seidel with SOR (ω≈1.9). η = η₀(1+4vf)·γ̇ⁿ⁻¹, γ̇ ≈ (6·|∇p|·h_gap)/(12η·h_gap) updated every 10 sweeps.
C · VOF frontFill fraction f∈[0,1] advanced with u = −(h²/12η)∇p using semi-Lagrangian flux upwinding per Δt. Air traps: unfilled cells whose 4-neighbors all become filled before they do.
Non-isoT(x,y,t): filled cells decay exponentially toward T_mold with τ=Cool τ; η scaled by exp(E/R·(1/T−1/T_ref)) (Arrhenius).
Curedα/dt = k(T)(1−α), k = k0·exp(−Ea/(R·T)), integrated per Δt over sim time.
Orient.Advancement of 2D tensor A by local velocity gradient + isotropic Folgar–Tucker relaxation: A ← A + Δt[(∇v)·A + A·(∇v)ᵀ − 2ClĠ(I/2? − A)] (clamped to eigenvalues ≥0, trace 1). View shows A11−A22 anisotropy.
WeldCell reached simultaneously from ≥2 fronts ⇒ weld. Strength ≈ exp(−Δt_front/τ_w): larger arrival-time difference ⇒ weaker joint.
ClampF = Σ p·A_cell over cavity projected to kN; includes squeeze-flow term 3ηV R²/(2h³) order estimate.
